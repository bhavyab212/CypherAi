Proof & Methodology: the Ledger Behind /seo/

By Priyansh Razz, Founder and AI automation specialist · Published 12 October 2026 · Updated 12 October 2026

This page is the warehouse behind the AI SEO services page: how each claim is sourced, how often it expires, and the changelog proving it. Targets are labeled targets, processes are labeled processes, and rows graduate to measured data only after a dated verification run.

Methodology: how a ledger row is verified

Every row resolves to exactly one of three checks. A live tool run means PageSpeed, the Schema.org validator, or Search Console field data, with the run date in the row. A site page means recent work or contact showing the practice in production. A dated commitment means a process promise — quote in 48 hours, 45-minute kickoff — you can hold us to in writing before paying. If a row cannot resolve to one of the three, it does not ship.

Freshness SLAs: when rows expire

Stack and integration rows (C-03, C-04) are re-confirmed quarterly. Speed rows (C-02) are re-run after every template, hosting, or script change. Schema rows (C-05) are re-validated at every publish. Process rows (C-01, C-06) are reviewed annually. Expired rows are flagged in place with their old date until re-verified — never silently deleted — so this page stays an auditable history, not a brochure.
