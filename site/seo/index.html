What do AI SEO services include at CypherAi?

Our AI SEO services cover four layers in one engagement: the technical audit, GEO and content operations, on-page optimization, and SEO automation. The audit spans crawlability, indexability, canonicals, robots, sitemaps, redirects, and JavaScript rendering, because pages that cannot be discovered or canonicalized correctly cannot rank no matter how good the copy is. GEO and content operations define your entity once — who you are, what you sell, which stack you run, how fast you deliver — and repeat it across pages, llms.txt, and schema, with quotable 40 to 120 word answers, comparison tables, and FAQ content written so answer engines can lift it with attribution. On-page work means one intent per page, unique titles of 30 to 60 characters, meta descriptions of 120 to 160 characters with a call to action, semantic headings, descriptive internal links, and alt text of 10 to 125 characters on every meaningful image. Automation closes the loop: briefs, internal linking, content refreshes, and reporting run as logged workflows instead of manual chores. How do n8n and Make workflows automate SEO at scale?

They turn SEO from a pile of recurring tickets into logged, scheduled pipelines. A content-brief workflow watches Search Console for slipping queries and drafts briefs with the target intent, headings, entities, and internal-link targets before a writer starts. An internal-linking workflow scans new pages for orphaned topics and proposes exact anchor placements. A refresh workflow flags pages whose facts, dates, or screenshots went stale and opens update tasks with the changed lines highlighted. A reporting workflow snapshots rankings, impressions, Core Web Vitals field data, and answer-engine citation probes into one monthly record in PostgreSQL, so no metric lives only in someone's head. Every run is logged, retryable, and reversible — the same workflow discipline as our client automation, applied to your search program. Targets stay concrete: LCP of 2.5 seconds or less, INP of 200 milliseconds or less, and CLS of 0.1 or less at the 75th percentile.

Which AI stack powers our SEO automation?

We build on GPT-4o, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, and open-source Llama models, orchestrated through n8n and Make, voiced via ElevenLabs and Twilio, and persisted in PostgreSQL. Model choice is per task, not per religion: GPT-4o-class models for drafting and extraction, Claude-class for long-form reasoning, Gemini where its context or cost fits, and Llama-based deployments where data must stay inside your boundary. Guardrails are non-negotiable: human approval on publishing and outbound actions, eval checks on extraction quality, and observability on every run so failures page someone instead of silently corrupting content. Schemas we ship validate — Organization, WebSite, Service, WebPage, BreadcrumbList, Person — and we never ship deprecated HowTo markup or FAQPage markup chasing rich results Google no longer awards to commercial sites. How long until we see results?

Technical fixes typically reflect in Search Console within two to six weeks of indexing. Competitive-term movement usually takes two to four months. AI-citation visibility often moves faster once entities and FAQ content are clean, because answer engines re-ingest structured pages quickly.

No, and distrust anyone who does. We guarantee the work surface: every Critical and High issue fixed, every page measured before and after, every claim reproducible from Search Console, PageSpeed, and schema validators.

Will AI search kill the value of Google SEO?

No. Answer engines cite pages that are crawlable, canonicalized, structured, fast, and trustworthy — the same foundation Google rewards. One investment serves both surfaces.
